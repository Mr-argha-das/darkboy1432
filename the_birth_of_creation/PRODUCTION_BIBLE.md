## 2. Mythological Accuracy & Cultural Integrity Notes

This documentary adheres strictly to the **Vaishnava / Puranic cosmic creation tradition** (primarily attested in the *Bhagavata Purana* [Skandha 3, Adhyaya 8], *Vishnu Purana*, and *Padma Purana*):
- **Primordial State & Ksheer Sagar:** Before manifestation (*Srishti*), Lord Vishnu (*Narayana*) rests in serene *Yoga Nidra* upon the infinite cosmic serpent **Ananta Shesha** floating upon the primordial cosmic ocean (*Ksheer Sagar*).
- **Padmanabha Motif:** From Lord Vishnu's navel emerges the radiant golden divine lotus (*Padma*), symbolizing cosmic potential unfolding out of stillness.
- **Chaturmukha Brahma:** Seated upon the unfurled lotus appears **Lord Brahma**, the four-faced creator deity holding the four *Vedas*, *Kamandalu*, *Akshamala* (prayer beads), and *Padma*, who first contemplates his own origin (*"Main kaun hoon?"*) before initiating cosmic creation.
- **Saptarishis & Prajapatis:** From Brahma's mind arise the seven great sages (*Saptarishis*) and *Prajapatis*, carrying forward knowledge, life, and consciousness.
- **The Eternal Cosmic Cycle (Trimurti):** Creation (*Srishti* — Brahma), Preservation (*Sthiti* — Vishnu), and Dissolution/Renewal (*Pralaya* — Shiva) form a timeless philosophical cycle rather than a single linear endpoint.
- **Framing Discipline:** The script explicitly frames events as Hindu Puranic philosophy and sacred tradition (*"Hindu Puranic parampara mein"*, *"Puranic varnan ke anusaar"*, *"Hindu darshan mein"*), never conflating mythology with experimental science.
